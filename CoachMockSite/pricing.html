Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing and plans

Can I change plans anytime?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll be charged the prorated difference immediately. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at your next billing cycle.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime with no penalties. Your account remains active until the end of your billing period, and you'll retain access to all your data. After cancellation, you'll be moved to the free Starter plan.

Is there really a 30-day money-back guarantee?

Absolutely! If you're not completely satisfied within 30 days of your first paid subscription, contact our support team for a full refund. No questions asked, no hoops to jump through.

Do you offer discounts for students or nonprofits?

Yes! We offer 50% discounts for verified students and registered nonprofits. Contact our sales team with your verification documents to get started.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and for Enterprise customers, we can arrange wire transfers or ACH payments.

Can I use IdeaCoach Pro for multiple businesses?

Professional and Enterprise plans include unlimited business projects. You can work on multiple ideas simultaneously and switch between them seamlessly.

Is my data secure and private?

Yes! We use enterprise-grade security with 256-bit encryption, SOC 2 compliance, and GDPR compliance. Your business ideas and data are completely private and never shared.

What if I need features not included in any plan?

Enterprise customers can request custom features and integrations. We also have a roadmap driven by user feedback - contact us to discuss your specific needs.
