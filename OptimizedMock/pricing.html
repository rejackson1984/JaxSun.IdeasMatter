Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BuildLab pricing

Can I change plans anytime?

Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you'll get immediate access to new features and be charged the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel anytime with no penalties. Your account remains active until the end of your billing period, and you keep access to everything you've created. After cancellation, you'll be moved to the free Starter plan.

Is there really a 30-day guarantee?

Yes! If you're not completely satisfied within 30 days of your first paid subscription, we'll give you a full refund. No questions asked, no hoops to jump through.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes! We offer 50% discounts for verified students and educators. Contact our support team with your .edu email or student ID to get started.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and Apple Pay. For Enterprise customers, we can arrange wire transfers or ACH payments.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely! We use enterprise-grade security with 256-bit encryption, SOC 2 compliance, and GDPR compliance. Your business ideas and data are completely private and never shared.
